What is 벅뚜벅뚜?

Other maps have already drawn the whole world. The map in 벅뚜벅뚜 starts as a blank sheet of paper, and it fills in one dot at a time only when you walk. Roads you travel by car or subway aren't drawn.

  • A map that grows as you walk — every place you pass is stamped onto the paper. Paths you take often grow darker, and ones you haven't revisited in three months fade to grey
  • Paths you've drawn — what counts isn't distance walked but the length of new paths. Walk the same road a hundred times and the map doesn't grow
  • Stamps — stand where you are and press one of 212 stamps. Snap a shop sign with the camera and that sign stands on your map
  • Visited and want-to-visit places — spots you haven't been to stay as dotted stamps, and they firm up into visited places once you arrive
  • Pioneer Compass — points to the direction that's still white from where you stand
  • Film — export a video of your map being drawn
  • Only in your iCloud — your steps, diary, and photos live in your personal iCloud, out of anyone else's sight. Not a single coordinate is sent to the developer
